Quick answers

How do I add a Ditto widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Ditto, pick a size and tap Add Widget. Touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to pick which design it shows.

A widget isn't updating

Open Ditto once; widgets refresh when the app saves. If it still looks stale, remove the widget and add it again.

Does Ditto sync between my devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud, when you're signed in to iCloud on each device. There's no Ditto account.

How do I restore or cancel Ditto Pro?

In Ditto, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases or Manage Subscription. You can also cancel in your Apple Account's subscription settings.

I deleted something by mistake

Open Settings → Recently Deleted. Items stay there for 30 days.

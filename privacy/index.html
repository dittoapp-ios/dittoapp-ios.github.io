Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 Developer: Abhay Madala, an individual developer ("I", "me") Contact: dittoapp.support@gmail.com

Ditto is a reminders and Home Screen widget app for iPhone. It's designed so that your reminders stay yours. I don't run servers for Ditto, I don't have a Ditto account system, and I can't see your reminders.

1. Summary

  • Ditto has no account or login.

  • Your spaces and reminders are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they are also stored in your own private iCloud database, run by Apple. I have no access to that database.

  • Ditto contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. It doesn't use any third-party code.

  • Apple Reminders, notifications and photos are used only when you choose to use them, and only on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. I don't receive your payment details.

2. Information Ditto stores, and where

Information Where it's stored Shared with me?
Spaces: name, order, color/look, sort setting On your device; your private iCloud if iCloud is on No
Reminders: text, completion, order, due date/time, notes, priority, alert choice, deletion date On your device; your private iCloud if iCloud is on No
Widget designs: name, chosen spaces and their order, navigation style On your device; your private iCloud if iCloud is on No
Photos and wallpaper images you choose for backgrounds and transparent widgets On your device only (not synced) No
Links to Apple Reminders you add to a space, with a copy of their title, notes, dates, priority and completion On your device only (not synced by Ditto) No
App settings (appearance, alerts on/off, Apple Reminders on/off, onboarding completed, a random per-install device identifier, iCloud account change markers) On your device only No
Safety copies of Ditto's local database, made before the first iCloud sync and before syncing with a different iCloud account On your device only No

The per-install device identifier is a random value created on your device. Ditto uses it only to tell apart transparent widget setups made on your different devices. It is never sent anywhere except inside your own widget designs in your private iCloud.

3. iCloud (CloudKit)

If you're signed in to iCloud, Ditto automatically syncs your spaces, reminders and widget designs through Apple's iCloud, using your private CloudKit database. This data is protected by your Apple Account, and I can't access it. If iCloud isn't available, Ditto keeps working with the data on your device. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Images (photos and wallpapers) and Apple Reminders links are not synced through Ditto's iCloud data.

4. Apple Reminders (EventKit)

Apple Reminders integration is off by default. Ditto asks for Reminders access only when you turn it on in Settings. With access, Ditto reads your reminders so you can choose which to show in a space. It then keeps the reminders you added in sync: title, notes, due date and time, priority, and completion.

Ditto never deletes reminders from Apple Reminders. "Remove from Space" removes only Ditto's link.

Reminders data stays on your device and in Apple's own Reminders service. You can turn off access at any time in the iPhone's Settings app.

5. Notifications

If you turn on an alert for a reminder, Ditto asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. These notifications are not sent through any server of mine.

Ditto also registers for Apple's push notifications only so that iCloud can tell the app when your data changed on another device. I don't send any notification content.

6. Photos

When you choose a background or wallpaper image, you pick it with Apple's photo picker. Ditto receives only the image you chose and saves a resized copy on your device.

7. Purchases (Ditto Pro)

Ditto Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Ditto receives confirmation of your purchase from Apple on your device, to unlock Pro. I don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Apple provides developers with aggregated sales and subscription reports.

8. Information Apple may share with me

If you've agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give me aggregated app usage statistics and crash reports. These don't include your reminders. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

9. Data I collect

I do not collect personal data through Ditto. I don't sell, rent or share your data, and I don't use it for advertising or tracking.

If you contact me by email, I'll receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. I use it only to reply and to fix problems, and I delete support emails within 12 months once the conversation is finished. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Recently Deleted: deleted reminders and spaces move to Recently Deleted. You can restore them for 30 days. After 30 days they're permanently deleted the next time you open Ditto. You can also delete them permanently sooner from Recently Deleted.

  • Deleting the app removes Ditto's data stored on that device, including images and safety copies.

  • iCloud data stays in your iCloud until you delete it. You can do that in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, where data saved by apps is listed by app.

  • If Pro ends, nothing is deleted.

11. Security

Your data is protected by your device's security and, for iCloud, by your Apple Account. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but Ditto sends no data to my servers, because there are none.

12. Children

Ditto is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • use Ditto without iCloud, Apple Reminders, notifications or photos;
  • turn off access in the iPhone's Settings app at any time;
  • delete any data in the app or by deleting the app.

Because I hold none of your data, requests to access or delete it are handled on your device and in your iCloud. You can still contact me with any privacy question.

Depending on where you live (for example in the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to complain to a data-protection authority. The only personal data I can hold is support email you send me; email me and I'll answer or delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.

Ditto Pro — Subscription Terms

Provided by: Abhay Madala · Contact: dittoapp.support@gmail.com

What Ditto Pro includes

  • Unlimited spaces.
  • Wallpaper-matched transparent widgets.

Ditto Free includes:

  • unlimited reminders and three spaces;
  • every widget size and widget navigation;
  • solid colors, notifications, Apple Reminders and iCloud sync.

Plans

Plan Type U.S. price
Ditto Pro Monthly Auto-renewing subscription, billed every month $1.99
Ditto Pro Yearly Auto-renewing subscription, billed every year $9.99
Ditto Pro Lifetime One-time purchase, doesn't renew $14.99

Prices in other countries and currencies are set by the App Store and shown before you buy. Monthly and Yearly are in one subscription group, so you can have only one of them at a time.

Payment and automatic renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.

Cancelling

Cancel any time in your Apple Account's subscription settings, or in Ditto: Settings → Manage Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

When Pro ends

Nothing is deleted. Your spaces, reminders, widgets and saved wallpaper setups are kept and remain usable and editable. Creating more than three spaces and choosing transparency need Pro again. Transparent widgets show a solid color until Pro is active again.

Lifetime

Lifetime is a single purchase that keeps Pro active for as long as the App is offered. It isn't affected by any subscription ending.

If the App is ever discontinued, Lifetime keeps Pro unlocked in the version you have installed for as long as it runs on your device.

Restoring purchases

Purchases belong to your Apple Account. On a new device, use the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the upgrade screen.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies (reportaproblem.apple.com). If Apple refunds a purchase, Pro ends and your data is kept.

Family Sharing

Ditto Pro isn't shared through Family Sharing.
